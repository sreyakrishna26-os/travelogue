03 / TRAVEL NOTES

Know the destinations.

Some special places worth exploring on your Kerala journey.

01

Munnar – The Green Paradise

Munnar is known for its rolling tea plantations, cool climate and scenic mountain views. Popular attractions include Eravikulam National Park, Mattupetty Dam and Echo Point.

02

Alleppey – The Backwater Beauty

Alleppey is famous for its peaceful canals, houseboat experiences and beautiful backwater landscapes. It is a wonderful place to experience Kerala's traditional village life.

03

Wayanad – Into the Wild

Wayanad offers green landscapes, forests, waterfalls and scenic viewpoints. Edakkal Caves, Pookode Lake and Banasura Sagar Dam are popular attractions.

04

Thekkady – Nature and Wildlife

Thekkady is a beautiful destination for nature lovers. Explore Periyar Wildlife Sanctuary, enjoy spice plantation visits and discover the region's rich biodiversity.

05

Varkala – Where the Sea Meets the Sky

Varkala is famous for its dramatic coastal cliffs, beaches and beautiful sunset views. It is a relaxing destination for anyone who loves the sea.

06

Kochi – A City of Stories

Kochi brings together history, art and modern city life. Explore Fort Kochi, the Chinese fishing nets, heritage streets and local cafes.
